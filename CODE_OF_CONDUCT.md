# Code of conduct

Chirograph is a technical collaboration project. Participants are expected to make it possible for people to disagree about software, evidence, design, and benchmarks without turning the disagreement into hostility toward the people involved.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims and implementations with concrete evidence.
- Be specific about uncertainty, assumptions, and reproducibility.
- Respect different levels of experience and different communication styles.
- Keep reviews focused on the contribution and its consequences.
- Accept correction when stronger evidence changes the answer.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate intimidation, sexualized harassment, sustained personal attacks, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose a target of harassment or other private information.

This code of conduct applies in repository discussions and other spaces where someone is acting on behalf of the Chirograph project.
